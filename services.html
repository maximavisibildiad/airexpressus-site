Services

Aire Express Ohio LLC is licensed and insured in the state of Ohio.

Full System Installation

Complete installation of air conditioning and heating systems — including central air, furnaces, and heat pumps — for residential and commercial properties. This is our core line of work and typically starts with an on-site estimate so we can give you an accurate, full project quote before any work begins.

Repairs

If your system isn't cooling, heating, or running the way it should, we diagnose the issue and repair air conditioning units, furnaces, and heat pumps.

Maintenance

Routine maintenance and seasonal tune-ups to keep your system running efficiently and to catch small issues before they become expensive repairs.

Our Process

1. Reach Out

Call, text, or fill out our contact form with what's going on or what you're looking to install.

2. On-Site Estimate

For installations, we schedule a visit to assess the job and put together a full, accurate quote — no guessing over the phone.

3. Scheduled Work

Once you approve the quote, we schedule the installation, repair, or maintenance visit at a time that works for you.

4. Completed & Tested

We test the system before we leave to make sure everything is running the way it should.

Service Area

We serve Columbus, Ohio and surrounding communities within approximately 150 miles, for both residential and commercial properties.
